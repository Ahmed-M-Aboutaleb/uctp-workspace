g++ -std=c++17 -O2 -I include/core -I include/algorithm -o gsnsga2 main.cpp

Single run:
./gsnsga2 data/socha/small1.txt 100 300

20 runs (variable seeds):
./gsnsga2 data/socha/small1.txt 100 300 --runs 20

20 runs (fixed seed):
./gsnsga2 data/socha/small1.txt 100 300 --runs 20 --fixed-seed

Single run with specific seed:
./gsnsga2 data/socha/small1.txt 100 300 --seed 49
